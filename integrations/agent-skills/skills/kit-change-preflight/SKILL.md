Follow `.engineering-kit/kit/procedures/change-preflight.md` step by step. It is the single source
of these steps and changes with the installed kit release. The contract in
`.engineering-kit/kit/CONTRACT.md` and this project's rules in
`.engineering-kit/project.json` and its instruction files also apply; where
they differ, the stricter one applies.
